The Trip, by Train

Erfurt to Rennes on one direct train, ten stops across Brittany, then home from Nantes — every leg on rails.

You decided to travel entirely by train: out to Rennes, home from Nantes, with the regional network linking the ten stops in between. The centrepiece is the daily direct ICE between Erfurt and Paris — six hours, no change between Germany and France. Here is the whole journey, leg by leg, with times and what it costs for the four of you.

Yes — there's a direct train

Since 14 December 2025 the daily Berlin–Paris ICE stops at Erfurt, so you ride straight from Erfurt to Paris with no change between Germany and France.

Erfurt → Paris 08:50 → 14:51 6 h 01 · ICE 9590, every day
Paris → Erfurt 11:07 → 17:10 6 h 03 · ICE 9591, every day

Book it as a single Sparpreis Europa ticket on bahn.de from €59.90 pp in 2nd class (€69.90 in 1st). It runs just once a day each way, and four cheap seats together in July go fast — reserve as early as you can. If it's full, an ICE change at Frankfurt gives hourly alternatives.

Booking & Practicalities

  • Two booking sites. Buy the Germany↔France ICE on bahn.de (one Sparpreis Europa ticket, Erfurt–Paris). Buy the TGV and regional trains on SNCF Connect. Both open about six months ahead, so July 2026 is bookable now.
  • Reserve the fast trains, not the regional ones. ICE and TGV need a seat reservation for a specific departure; TER / BreizhGo trains don't — just hop on.
  • The Paris change is across the city. Gare de l'Est and Gare Montparnasse are linked directly by Métro line 4 (~25 min); with four people and luggage a taxi (~€25–35, 20–30 min) is easier. The plan deliberately leaves about two hours either way — the hop itself is short, so the spare time is insurance against a late train, and above all against missing the once-a-day train home.
  • One direct train a day. The Erfurt–Paris ICE runs once each way (out 08:50, back 11:07). The early Nantes start on Day 13 is the price of catching it; if you'd rather sleep in, change at Frankfurt instead and reach Erfurt in the evening.
  • Re-check before you pay. These are the current (Dec 2025) times; always confirm the exact departures on the day you book, and reserve four seats together while the cheap fares last.
