Tides & weather

Brittany runs on the tide — check the day's times before any Grand Bé crossing or a Mont-Saint-Michel bay walk. Good news: no grandes marées fall in your window (the big spring tides peaked on 17 July, just before you arrive), so tides stay moderate — but the Grand Bé still cuts off at high water, so glance at the day's table first.

🚗 Teilauto — breakdown or accident
0345 44 5000 · from France dial +49 345 44 5000

Teilauto Servicezentrale (Halle) — for a breakdown, an accident, or a car that won't lock or start. After a crash: secure the scene, call 112 (or 15) for anyone hurt and 17 for the police, then phone Teilauto and note the other driver's details and the police reference. The green insurance card (Grüne Karte) lives in the Bordbuch in the glovebox. Confirm the exact number on the sticker in the car before you rely on it.

Check the car carries these (required in France)

  • Warning triangle (triangle de présignalisation) — place it at least 30 m behind the car.
  • Hi-vis vest (gilet jaune) — kept within reach inside the cabin, not the boot; ideally one per person.
  • First-aid kit & spare bulbs — not compulsory but recommended (usually standard in German cars).
  • Green insurance card (Grüne Karte) — in the Bordbuch/glovebox; keep it accessible for the whole trip.

Packliste — Bring from Home

  • Crit'Air sticker — ordered and stuck on the windscreen before you leave Germany.
  • Bath towels — some places don't supply them (Quiberon); bring your own to be safe.
  • €60 in cash — Axel pays this for the bed linen at the Quiberon flat, on arrival (it's already counted in the €352 above).
  • Tea towel (Abtrockentuch) and a dishcloth & sponge (Aufwaschlappen/Schwamm) for the self-catering apartments.
  • A card or télépéage badge for the autoroute tolls.
  • Light rain jacket & layers and proper shoes for the GR34 coast path — Breton weather turns fast.
